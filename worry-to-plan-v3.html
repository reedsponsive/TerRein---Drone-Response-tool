Worry to plan

Say what you are worried about, mark the area on the map, and get a drone survey plan.

Findings in reports are simulated. No drone flies and no images are analysed, so the report shows only what a finished one would look like. This page runs entirely in your browser and sends nothing anywhere.

No keep-out zones yet.

To avoid buildings: add a map image, choose Pick a roof colour, and click a few roofs. Matching shapes become red keep-out boxes, and the flight path goes around them with the margin shown by the dashed yellow outline. This is a colour match, not building recognition, so check the boxes. Choose Draw or delete keep-out boxes to drag a new box or click a box to remove it.

Click the map to add points. Drag a point to move it. Click a line to add a point on it. Double-click a point to delete it.

Live map tiles cannot load on this page. To see your real area, use Add map image with a square screenshot of it, then set Map width to the distance it covers. The image stays in your browser.

Aviation rules differ by country. Check yours before flying, and keep the drone in sight unless you hold approval.
